Federally listed amphibians of Alabama

Alabama has 2 amphibians on the federal list of endangered and threatened species: 1 listed as endangered and 1 as threatened. One more, the eastern hellbender, has been proposed for listing. Another 2 listed amphibians once lived here and are thought to be gone. Each entry says what the amphibian looks like, where it lives, why it is at risk, and where to read more.
