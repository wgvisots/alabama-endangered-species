All birds

Rufa red knot

Calidris canutus rufa

A stocky sandpiper that flies as far as 19,000 miles a year between the Arctic and the far south.

What it looks like

A stocky shorebird 9 to 11 inches long with a short black bill. In spring its face and underparts are rusty red. In winter it is pale gray above and white below.

Where it lives

Coastal bays, estuaries, and tidal flats. It nests on dry Arctic tundra near fresh water.

How it lives

It is among the longest-distance migrants known. It eats mostly small hard-shelled mollusks, and in spring the eggs of horseshoe crabs.

In Alabama

A bird of the coast in migration and in winter. The Service places part of its wintering range in the southeastern United States, from Alabama to North Carolina.

Why it is at risk

Too few safe places to rest at high tide, disturbance by people, and falcons. The recovery plan covers the wider threats.
