Federally listed birds of Alabama

Alabama has 5 birds on the federal list of endangered and threatened species: 4 listed as threatened and 1 as an experimental population. One listed bird, the ivory-billed woodpecker, once lived here and is thought to be gone. Each entry says what the bird looks like, where it lives, why it is at risk, and where to read more.

The strip beside a bird shows the months it is in Alabama, where a source gives them. The outlined square is this month.

The wood stork is no longer here. A rule published on February 10, 2026 removed it from the federal list.
