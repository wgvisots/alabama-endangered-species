Federally listed mammals of Alabama

Alabama has 6 mammals on the federal list of endangered and threatened species: 5 listed as endangered and 1 as threatened. One more, the tricolored bat, has been proposed for listing. Another 2 listed mammals once lived here and are thought to be gone. Each entry says what the mammal looks like, where it lives, why it is at risk, and where to read more.

The strip beside the manatee shows the months it is usually in Alabama waters. The outlined square is this month.

Whales and other animals of the open sea, which fall to NOAA Fisheries, are not covered here.
