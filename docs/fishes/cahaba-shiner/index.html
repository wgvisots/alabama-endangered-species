Cahaba shiner

Notropis cahabae

A small silvery minnow of the shoals of the Cahaba River.

What it looks like

A small, fairly stout minnow, 1.4 to 1.8 inches long, with large eyes. It is light olive above and silvery below, with a dark stripe along the side, and it takes on no bright breeding colors.

Where it lives

Shallow shoals of the main river, just downstream of riffles, over clean sand or sand and gravel, and shallow water running through beds of water plants.

How it lives

It spawns from the middle of May through early July, most of all in June.

In Alabama

It lives in the main channel of the Cahaba River, along about 60 miles from Perry County up to Shelby County. Most of the fish are in about 15 miles of river in Bibb County.

Why it is at risk

Falling water quality, chiefly from wastewater, and silt from mining, road building, and the clearing of drilling sites.
