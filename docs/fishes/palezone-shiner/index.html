Palezone shiner

Notropis albizonatus

A slender minnow with a pale stripe above a dark one, found in Alabama only in the Paint Rock River.

No photo here yet. This site only shows photos that are in the public domain or openly licensed, and none has been found for this fish. The Fish & Wildlife Service profile is the best place to look for one.

What it looks like

A slender, spindle-shaped minnow, 1.2 to 2.4 inches long, with a pointed snout and large eyes. A narrow dark band runs from snout to tail, with a pale stripe above it.

Where it lives

Clear, permanent upland creeks and small rivers, in runs and the flowing heads of pools, over clean bedrock, cobble, and gravel mixed with sand.

How it lives

It spawns from about the middle of May to early July, and lives three to four years.

In Alabama

It lives only in the upper Paint Rock River system, in Jackson County, along about 17 miles of stream.

Why it is at risk

Water pollution, dams, the straightening of streams, the loss of streamside trees, and silt. With only two populations, a drought or a spill could do great harm.
