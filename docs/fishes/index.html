Federally listed fishes of Alabama

Alabama has 15 fishes on the federal list of endangered and threatened species: 8 listed as endangered and 7 as threatened. One more, the coal darter, has been proposed for listing. One listed fish, the spotfin chub, once lived here and is thought to be gone. Each entry says what the fish looks like, where it lives, why it is at risk, and where to read more.

The snail darter is no longer here. It was removed from the federal list in 2022. Sawfish, sharks, and other fishes of the sea, which fall to NOAA Fisheries, are not covered.
