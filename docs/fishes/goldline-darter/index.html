Goldline darter

Percina aurolineata

A darter with a stripe of amber along its back, found in Alabama only in the Cahaba River system.

What it looks like

A darter 1.6 to 2.9 inches long. A bright amber or russet stripe runs from the head to the rear of the back, above a row of eight or nine dark blotches, and there are three dark spots at the base of the tail.

Where it lives

Shallow riffles with moderate to swift current in the main channels of free-flowing rivers, over cobble, small boulders, or bedrock, among water willow and river weed.

How it lives

It spawns from March into June.

In Alabama

Alabama's fish live in the middle Cahaba River system, including the Little Cahaba River.

Why it is at risk

The state's account ties falling numbers in the Little Cahaba to muddier water and silt from the upper part of the watershed.
