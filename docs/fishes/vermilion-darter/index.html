Vermilion darter

Etheostoma chermocki

A red-bellied darter whose whole world is the upper reach of one creek on the edge of Birmingham.

What it looks like

A darter 1.8 to 2.4 inches long. Breeding males are light olive to straw, with eight dark saddles across the back, brick-red spots along the side, and a dark vermilion belly. The front fin on the back carries a cherry-red eyespot.

Where it lives

Streams of moderate to swift flow, with riffles of limestone rubble and shale and with bedrock pools. Near springs, it keeps to swift runs beside watercress and pondweed.

In Alabama

Found only in upper Turkey Creek, in Jefferson County, a tributary of the Locust Fork of the Black Warrior River.

Why it is at risk

Silt from the spread of the city into its watershed.
