Leatherback sea turtle

Dermochelys coriacea

The largest of all turtles, a deep diver of the open sea that passes through the Gulf off Alabama.

What it looks like

The largest sea turtle, usually 4 to 8 feet long and 500 to 2,000 pounds. It has no hard shell. Its back is a mosaic of small bones under tough, rubbery skin, with seven raised ridges running its length. It is mostly black with pale flecks, and its paddle-like limbs have no claws.

Where it lives

The open sea.

How it lives

It dives deeper and travels farther than any other sea turtle.

In Alabama

Leatherbacks are found offshore, in the Gulf waters off Alabama. The state does not count it among the turtles that nest on its beaches.

Why it is at risk
