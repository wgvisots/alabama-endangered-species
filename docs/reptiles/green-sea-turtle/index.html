Green sea turtle

Chelonia mydas

A large sea turtle with a smooth, heart-shaped shell, and one of three kinds that nest on Alabama's beaches.

What it looks like

Adults can reach about 4 feet and 440 pounds. The shell is heart-shaped and smooth, brown with dark blotches, and the underside is pale. The head is small, and each flipper has a single claw. Hatchlings are black above and white below.

Where it lives

Coastal Gulf waters. The females nest on sandy beaches.

In Alabama

One of the three sea turtles that nest on Alabama's Gulf beaches, though far less often than the loggerhead.

Why it is at risk

On Alabama's beaches the chief danger is light, which confuses nesting females and draws hatchlings away from the water.
