American alligator

Alligator mississippiensis

No longer in danger, and kept on the federal list only because its hide looks like those of crocodilians that are.

Listed for its likeness, not its rarity. This reptile is on the federal list only because it is hard to tell from a species that is in danger.

No photo here yet. This site only shows photos that are in the public domain or openly licensed, and none has been found for this reptile. The Fish & Wildlife Service profile is the best place to look for one.

What it looks like

The sources read for this entry do not describe the alligator, and few Alabamians need it described.

Where it lives

The sources read for this entry do not describe its habitat.

In Alabama

The Encyclopedia of Alabama notes that the alligator is neither threatened nor endangered in the state. It stays on the federal list under a special rule, 50 CFR 17.42(a).

Why it is at risk

None that put it on the list today.

Notes

First listed in 1967, when it was in real danger.
