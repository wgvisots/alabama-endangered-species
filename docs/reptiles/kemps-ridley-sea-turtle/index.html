Kemp's ridley sea turtle

Lepidochelys kempii

The smallest sea turtle, a crab-eater of shallow Gulf waters that sometimes nests in Alabama.

What it looks like

The smallest of the sea turtles, about 2 feet long and 100 pounds. The adult has a nearly round, olive-gray shell and a triangular head with a hooked beak. Hatchlings are black above and below.

Where it lives

Shallow coastal waters, where it feeds along the bottom.

How it lives

It eats mostly crabs.

In Alabama

One of the three sea turtles that nest on Alabama's Gulf beaches, though far less often than the loggerhead.

Why it is at risk

On Alabama's beaches the chief danger is light, which confuses nesting females and draws hatchlings away from the water. The five-year review covers the wider threats.
