All mussels

Scaleshell

Leptodea leptodon

A mussel once found in the Tennessee River at Muscle Shoals, and gone from Alabama.

Thought to be gone from Alabama. This mussel is on the federal list and once lived in the state, but it is not known here today.

No photo here yet. This site only shows photos that are in the public domain or openly licensed, and none has been found for this mussel.
