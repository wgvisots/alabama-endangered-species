All mussels

Ovate clubshell

Pleurobema perovatum

A small, oval mussel that once lived through most of the Mobile basin and now holds a handful of streams.

No photo here yet. This site only shows photos that are in the public domain or openly licensed, and none has been found for this mussel.

What it looks like

A small to medium, oval shell, rarely over 2 inches long, yellow to dark brown, sometimes with green rays, and white inside.

Where it lives

Shoals and runs of sand and gravel in small rivers and large streams.

In Alabama

It once lived in the Tombigbee, Black Warrior, Alabama, Cahaba, and Coosa systems. The recovery plan of 2000 knew it from the Sipsey River in Greene, Pickens, and Tuscaloosa Counties, from the Sucarnoochee River in Sumter County, from single creeks in Pickens and Macon Counties, and from the Coosa River in Cherokee County.

Why it is at risk

Dams buried its shoals under still water and cut its populations off from one another. Dredging, channel work, and gravel mining destroyed more, and runoff from mines, towns, and farms fouls what is left.
