All mussels

Southern sandshell

Hamiota australis

A smooth, shining mussel of soft stream bottoms in three river systems of south Alabama.

No photo here yet. This site only shows photos that are in the public domain or openly licensed, and none has been found for this mussel.

What it looks like

An elliptical, smooth, shiny shell. Young ones are greenish, and old ones dark greenish brown to black, often with green rays. The inside is bluish white.

Where it lives

Soft bottoms in streams with at least some current.

In Alabama

It lives in the Conecuh, Yellow, and Choctawhatchee river systems, in Barbour, Coffee, Conecuh, Covington, Crenshaw, Dale, Geneva, and Pike Counties.

Why it is at risk

Silt, shifting stream beds, and polluted water, above all. Dams, gravel mining, dredging, and the clearing of snags add to the harm, and floods and droughts fall hard on populations this small.
