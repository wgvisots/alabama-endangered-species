All mussels

Inflated heelsplitter

Potamilus inflatus

A large, thin-shelled mussel with a high wing, at home in the soft bottoms of the Black Warrior and Tombigbee.

No photo here yet. This site only shows photos that are in the public domain or openly licensed, and none has been found for this mussel. The Fish & Wildlife Service profile is the best place to look for one.

What it looks like

A large, thin, oval shell, up to about 6 inches long, with a prominent wing at the rear. It is brown to black outside, and pink to purple inside. Young ones may have green rays.

Where it lives

Soft, stable bottoms of sand, mud, silt, or sandy gravel, in slow to moderate current.

How it lives

The freshwater drum is the only fish known to carry its larvae.

In Alabama

It lives in the Black Warrior River up into Tuscaloosa County, in the Tombigbee River from its mouth upstream, and in the lower Sipsey River in Greene County. A single animal has been found in the lower Alabama River.

Why it is at risk

The profile read for this entry does not list threats.
