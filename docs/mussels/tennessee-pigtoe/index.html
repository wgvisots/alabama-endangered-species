All mussels

Tennessee pigtoe

Pleuronaia barnesiana

A yellowish-brown mussel of north Alabama creeks, proposed for listing as endangered.

Proposed, not listed. This mussel has no protection under the Endangered Species Act unless a final rule lists it.

No photo here yet. This site only shows photos that are in the public domain or openly licensed, and none has been found for this mussel.

What it looks like

An oval to somewhat triangular or squarish shell, up to about 3.7 inches long, yellowish or brown, sometimes with dark green rays.

Where it lives

Moderate current, and seldom pools, from small streams to large rivers, over sand, gravel, and cobble.

In Alabama

The state's list finds it in a few tributaries in north Alabama, and gone from the Tennessee River itself.

Why it is at risk

Large reservoirs, which change the flow and drown the riffles; the growth of towns; the mining of coal and the drilling of oil and gas; and farming.
