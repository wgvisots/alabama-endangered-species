All mussels

Black clubshell

Pleurobema curtum

A mussel of the Tombigbee River that has not been found alive anywhere since 1997.

Thought to be gone from Alabama. This mussel is on the federal list and once lived in the state, but it is not known here today.

No photo here yet. This site only shows photos that are in the public domain or openly licensed, and none has been found for this mussel.

Where it lives

The sources read for this entry do not describe its habitat.

In Alabama

The state's list records it from the Tombigbee River and counts it as gone from Alabama.

Why it is at risk

The destruction of its river by dams and the still water behind them.
