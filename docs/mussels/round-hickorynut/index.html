All mussels

Round hickorynut

Obovaria subrotunda

A small to medium mussel that has lost three-quarters of its known populations, and holds on in Alabama in the Paint Rock.

No photo here yet. This site only shows photos that are in the public domain or openly licensed, and none has been found for this mussel. The Fish & Wildlife Service profile is the best place to look for one.

What it looks like

A small to medium mussel, up to 3 inches long. It can live 15 years.

Where it lives

Small streams to large rivers, on bottoms of mixed sand, gravel, and cobble.

In Alabama

The state's list finds remnant populations only in the Paint Rock River system. Alabama is one of eight states where the Service has named critical habitat for it.

Why it is at risk

The loss and decline of its habitat, the isolation of its populations, and invading species. Of 297 populations once known, 69 are left, and 49 of those are in poor condition.
