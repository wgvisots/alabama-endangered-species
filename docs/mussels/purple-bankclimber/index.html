All mussels

Purple bankclimber

Elliptoideus sloatianus

A very large, heavy, sculptured mussel of big rivers, which touches Alabama only along the Chattahoochee.

No photo here yet. This site only shows photos that are in the public domain or openly licensed, and none has been found for this mussel.

What it looks like

A very large, heavy shell, up to about 8 inches long, strongly sculptured, with a well-marked ridge toward the rear. The inside is whitish at the center and deep purple toward the edge.

Where it lives

The larger streams of its river systems.

In Alabama

The state's list places it in Alabama or its border waters. The recovery plan of 2003 knew of a single site on the Chattahoochee River, which forms the line with Georgia.

Why it is at risk

Dams, channel work, pollution, and silt took most of its habitat. Erosion from the land, the drawing off of water, and spills still threaten it, and the populations left are small, cut off, and raising few young.
