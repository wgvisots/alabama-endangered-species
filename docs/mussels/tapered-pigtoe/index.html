All mussels

Tapered pigtoe

Fusconaia burkei

A ridged mussel found only in the Choctawhatchee River system.

No photo here yet. This site only shows photos that are in the public domain or openly licensed, and none has been found for this mussel.

What it looks like

An elliptical to somewhat triangular shell, up to about 3 inches long, with prominent ridges. Young shells are greenish to yellowish brown, and old ones dark brown to black.

Where it lives

Streams with at least some current.

In Alabama

It lives only in the Choctawhatchee River system, in Barbour, Coffee, Dale, Geneva, and Pike Counties.

Why it is at risk

Silt, shifting stream beds, and polluted water, above all. Dams, gravel mining, dredging, and the clearing of snags add to the harm, and floods and droughts fall hard on populations this small.
