All mussels

Orangefoot pimpleback

Plethobasus cooperianus

A mussel of deep, steadily flowing water over the shoals of big rivers.

No photo here yet. This site only shows photos that are in the public domain or openly licensed, and none has been found for this mussel. The Fish & Wildlife Service profile is the best place to look for one.

Where it lives

Riffles and shoals of sand, gravel, and cobble in medium to large rivers, mostly in deep water with a steady current.

In Alabama

It is on the federal list for Alabama as a mussel of the Tennessee River. The sources read for this entry do not say where in the river it survives.

Why it is at risk

Chiefly the dams. The shoals of the Tennessee River, where it lived, now lie under reservoirs, and what is left of its range is broken into pieces. Silt and pollution press on the survivors.
