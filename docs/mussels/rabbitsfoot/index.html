All mussels

Rabbitsfoot

Quadrula cylindrica cylindrica

A long, rectangular mussel marked with dark green chevrons, which lies on top of the gravel instead of burrowing.

No photo here yet. This site only shows photos that are in the public domain or openly licensed, and none has been found for this mussel. The Fish & Wildlife Service profile is the best place to look for one.

What it looks like

A medium to large shell, long and rectangular, yellowish, greenish, or olive, darkening with age. It is often patterned with dark green or nearly black chevrons and triangles. The inside is white, and the animal itself is usually orange.

Where it lives

Small to medium streams and some larger rivers, on bottoms of sand and gravel, where it lies on the surface.

In Alabama

The Service names three Alabama waters: the Paint Rock River, the Elk River, and Bear Creek.

Why it is at risk

Falling water quality, altered flows, and the breaking up of its habitat. Reservoirs have cut its populations off from one another.
