All mussels

Longsolid

Fusconaia subrotunda

A mussel that can live 50 years, and has lost more than half of its known populations.

No photo here yet. This site only shows photos that are in the public domain or openly licensed, and none has been found for this mussel.

What it looks like

A medium-sized mussel, up to 5 inches long. It can live 50 years.

Where it lives

Small streams to large rivers, on bottoms of mixed sand, gravel, and cobble.

In Alabama

Alabama is one of the states where it survives, and one of six where the Service has named critical habitat for it. The sources read for this entry do not name its Alabama streams.

Why it is at risk

The loss and decline of its habitat, the isolation of its populations, and invading species. Of 160 populations once known, 60 are left, and 48 of those are small and in poor condition.
