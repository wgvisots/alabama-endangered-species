All mussels

Ring pink

Obovaria retusa

A mussel of gravel and sand bars in big rivers, which may or may not still live below Wilson Dam.

No photo here yet. This site only shows photos that are in the public domain or openly licensed, and none has been found for this mussel.

Where it lives

Medium to large rivers, on bars of gravel and sand, usually in flowing water.

In Alabama

The state's list calls it rare and says only that remnant populations may survive below Wilson Dam.

Why it is at risk

Chiefly the dams. The shoals of the Tennessee River, where it lived, now lie under reservoirs, and what is left of its range is broken into pieces. Silt and pollution press on the survivors.
