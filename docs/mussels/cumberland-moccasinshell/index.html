All mussels

Cumberland moccasinshell

Medionidus conradicus

A small, green-rayed mussel of strong riffles, proposed for listing as endangered.

Proposed, not listed. This mussel has no protection under the Endangered Species Act unless a final rule lists it.

No photo here yet. This site only shows photos that are in the public domain or openly licensed, and none has been found for this mussel.

What it looks like

An elliptical, slightly arched shell, up to about 2.4 inches long, yellowish to tawny or brown, usually with green rays.

Where it lives

Riffles and shoals with strong current, from headwater streams to medium rivers, over gravel, cobble, and boulders, sometimes under flat rocks.

In Alabama

The proposal counts Alabama among the five states where it lives. It does not name the streams.

Why it is at risk

Large reservoirs, which change the flow and drown the riffles; the growth of towns; the mining of coal and the drilling of oil and gas; and farming.
