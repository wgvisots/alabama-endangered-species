All mussels

Snuffbox

Epioblasma triquetra

A thick, swollen, triangular mussel whose Alabama stronghold is the Paint Rock River.

No photo here yet. This site only shows photos that are in the public domain or openly licensed, and none has been found for this mussel. The Fish & Wildlife Service profile is the best place to look for one.

What it looks like

A small to medium mussel with a thick, much swollen shell. Males reach about 2.8 inches and are oblong or oval. Females reach about 1.8 inches and are roughly triangular. Young shells are yellowish to yellow-green and darken with age.

Where it lives

Shoals.

In Alabama

It once lived across north Alabama. The listing rule of 2012 knew it from the Paint Rock River, where the population was large and improving, and from small populations in the Tennessee and Elk Rivers. The state's list finds it only in the Paint Rock.

Why it is at risk

Dams and reservoirs, which break up its habitat, along with pollution, spills, and zebra mussels.
