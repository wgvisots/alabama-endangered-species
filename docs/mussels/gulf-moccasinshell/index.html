All mussels

Gulf moccasinshell

Medionidus penicillatus

A small, green-rayed mussel whose last Alabama home is a single creek in Houston County.

No photo here yet. This site only shows photos that are in the public domain or openly licensed, and none has been found for this mussel.

What it looks like

A small, thin shell, about 2.2 inches long, long and somewhat swollen, yellowish to greenish brown with fine green rays.

Where it lives

The main channels and tributaries of its rivers.

In Alabama

It once lived in the Chattahoochee River in Russell County and in several creeks there and in Houston County. Those are gone, and what remains in Alabama is in one creek in Houston County.

Why it is at risk

Dams, channel work, pollution, and silt took most of its habitat. Erosion from the land, the drawing off of water, and spills still threaten it, and the populations left are small, cut off, and raising few young.
