All mussels

White wartyback

Plethobasus cicatricosus

A thick, knobbed mussel whose only current records are from the Tennessee River below Wilson and Pickwick Dams.

No photo here yet. This site only shows photos that are in the public domain or openly licensed, and none has been found for this mussel. The Fish & Wildlife Service profile is the best place to look for one.

What it looks like

A thick, solid shell with a row of bumps or knobs running across it on a slant.

Where it lives

Thought to live on the shoals and riffles of large rivers.

In Alabama

It was once more widespread in the Ohio, Cumberland, and Tennessee river systems. Its only current records are from the lower Tennessee River, downstream of Wilson and Pickwick Dams.

Why it is at risk

Chiefly the dams. The shoals of the Tennessee River, where it lived, now lie under reservoirs, and what is left of its range is broken into pieces. Silt and pollution press on the survivors.
