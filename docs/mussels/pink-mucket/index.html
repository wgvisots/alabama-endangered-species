All mussels

Pink mucket

Lampsilis abrupta

A big-river mussel that survives in the swift water below the dams of the Tennessee River.

No photo here yet. This site only shows photos that are in the public domain or openly licensed, and none has been found for this mussel.

Where it lives

Strong current over rock and boulders, usually in water up to about three feet deep, and deeper, slower water over sand and gravel.

In Alabama

The state's list places it in the tailwaters of the dams on the Tennessee River and in a short reach of Bear Creek, in Colbert County.

Why it is at risk

Chiefly the dams. The shoals of the Tennessee River, where it lived, now lie under reservoirs, and what is left of its range is broken into pieces. Silt and pollution press on the survivors.
