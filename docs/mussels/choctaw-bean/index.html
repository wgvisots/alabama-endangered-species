All mussels

Choctaw bean

Obovaria choctawensis

A small, shiny, green-rayed mussel of three river systems of south Alabama.

No photo here yet. This site only shows photos that are in the public domain or openly licensed, and none has been found for this mussel. The Fish & Wildlife Service profile is the best place to look for one.

What it looks like

A small, oval shell, up to about 2 inches long, shiny greenish-brown, usually with thin green rays, and bluish white to smoky brown inside.

Where it lives

Streams with at least some current.

In Alabama

It lives in the Conecuh system in Conecuh, Crenshaw, and Pike Counties; in the Yellow River system in Covington County; and in the Choctawhatchee system in Barbour, Bullock, Coffee, Dale, Geneva, and Henry Counties.

Why it is at risk

Silt, shifting stream beds, and polluted water, above all. Dams, gravel mining, dredging, and the clearing of snags add to the harm, and floods and droughts fall hard on populations this small.
