Federally listed mussels of Alabama

Alabama has 42 mussels on the federal list of endangered and threatened species: 30 listed as endangered and 12 as threatened. Another 5 have been proposed for listing. Another 16 listed mussels once lived here and are thought to be gone. Each entry says what the mussel looks like, where it lives, why it is at risk, and where to read more.

A freshwater mussel begins life as a larva that must ride for a time on a fish, so a mussel is lost wherever its host fish is shut out. Many of these entries rest on recovery plans and state lists that are some years old. A few say nothing of the shell, because no source read for them describes it.
