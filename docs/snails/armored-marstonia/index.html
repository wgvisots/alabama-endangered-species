All snails

Armored marstonia

Marstonia pachyta

A tiny snail of submerged roots and mosses, found only in two creek systems of Limestone County.

No photo here yet. This site only shows photos that are in the public domain or openly licensed, and none has been found for this snail.

What it looks like

The sources read for this entry do not describe the shell. It is one of the pebblesnails, a family of very small snails.

Where it lives

It is most plentiful in underwater masses of tree roots and in mosses along the edges of creeks. It also lives on rocks, sunken leaves and wood, and water plants.

In Alabama

It lives only in the Limestone Creek and Piney Creek watersheds of Limestone County. Where it occurs it can be common.

Why it is at risk

Above all, falling water quality: silt, and runoff carrying fertilizer and other pollutants. Wheeler Dam has cut its creeks off from one another, and drought, flood, and invading plants and mussels add to the risk.
