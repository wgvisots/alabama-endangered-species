Federally listed insects of Alabama

Alabama has one insect protected under the federal Endangered Species Act: Mitchell's satyr, listed as endangered. One more, the monarch, has been proposed for listing. One listed insect, the American burying beetle, once lived here and is thought to be gone. Each entry says what the insect looks like, where it lives, why it is at risk, and where to read more.

The strip beside an insect shows the months its adults fly in Alabama, where a source gives them. The outlined square is this month.
