Louisiana quillwort

Isoetes louisianensis

A grass-like relative of the ferns that grows on sandbars in small streams.

No photo here yet. This site only shows photos that are in the public domain or openly licensed, and none has been found for this plant. The Fish & Wildlife Service profile is the best place to look for one.

What it looks like

A small, semi-aquatic plant that looks like a tuft of grass. Its slender, hollow, quill-like leaves can reach about 40 cm long and 2 to 3 mm wide, and spiral out from a two-lobed base. It makes no flowers; the spores form inside the widened bases of the leaves.

Where it grows

Sand and gravel bars and wet banks of small to medium streams, where the plants are often under water after rain.

In Alabama

Reported from Conecuh, Covington, Mobile, Monroe, and Washington Counties.

Why it is at risk

Its very small range is the main reason it was listed. The recovery plan covers threats site by site.
