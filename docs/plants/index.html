Federally listed plants of Alabama

Alabama has 23 plants on the federal list of endangered and threatened species: 14 listed as endangered and 9 as threatened. Each entry says what the plant looks like, where it grows, why it is at risk, and where to read more.

The strip beside each plant shows the months it flowers. A filled square is a month in flower. The outlined square is this month.
